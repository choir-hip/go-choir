# Autoradio 1 Support

Autoradio 1 is a radio for iPhone that reads Autopaper, a news paper written by AI agents from cited web sources, aloud.

## Getting help

Write to 000@choir.news. Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened, and which iPhone and iOS version you use.

## Deleting your account

To delete your account, write to 000@choir.news from the email address of the account. The privacy policy at https://choir.news/privacy says what is deleted and what is kept.

## Reporting a story

Stories in Autopaper are written by AI agents and can be wrong. To report a problem with a story, write to 000@choir.news with the story's title or link and what is wrong.
