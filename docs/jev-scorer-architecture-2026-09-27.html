Precommitment fabric + Jev scoring — architecture

Internal working diagram · 2026-09-27 · rev 4 (theory-aligned) · mutation class RED · pending owner ratification

Theory anchor (docs/precommitment-records-theory.md): the closed loop is the mechanism — in-context RL on one's own prediction errors is the learning signal. Agents receive their own scores and can read any score through the ObjectGraph read interface; the gated thing is only independent scores injected unmediated into context — the paper's rule is "keep them out, or sample and delay them." Scores are ledger facts, readable on demand; delivery to the owning desk is a status + ref, values behind a versioned visibility policy.

1 · Topology — authored contract, runtime settlement, management adjudication

Cells author via tray-staged verbs (μs, no network); rlm_reduce commits post-cell; the resolve commit mints the scoring obligation in the same conditional batch. All wakes ride the durable actor-wake outbox — wake is a hint, delivery is the pending-item drain at activation start.

2 · The five surfaces → the scorer battery

Jev supplies per-question judgments; proper-scoring-rule weighting and materiality math live in code. The battery maps to the theory's surfaces — correctness alone is the epistemic surface only.

SurfaceQuestion (Jev battery)Weight
Epistemic"Did the predicted outcome occur?" — noul/choice per consequencew(c)

Instrumental"Did the action cause the observed result?" — causal attributionw(c)
Procedural"Did the action stay within the action class / consequence envelope committed?" — say-dow(c)
NormativeNot scored by Jev — governance/authority surface; management adjudication + escalation—
w(c) = P(c|a) · impact(c) · relevance(c)  ·  coverage(P) = Σ covered w / Σ C* w  ·  score(P) = Σ_c∈C* w(c)·S(p_c, outcome_c)

Omitted consequences score as predicted-at-zero — omissions cost in proportion to materiality. Trivial predictions earn nothing (w=0). Catastrophe-listing loses — proper scoring penalizes high probs on non-events. Reach is the load-bearing materiality question: impact(change) ≈ max_consumers impact(consumer).

4 · Management as distributed supervisor

The paper's supervision topology, mapped to desks — a prediction market over behavior:

Paper roleDeskCommits on
Executor — predicts own action + outcomeEngineering / Research / TextureIts own work: the ordinary precommit path.
Critic — predicts what the executor will doManagementDesk behavior: "engineering's apply will pass verification" — frozen before, scored after, separately attributed.
Monitor — predicts material side effectsManagement (coverage) + criticsCoverage/omissions: "this resolve leaves consequence X unexamined" — adjudication records.

Joint consistency is the test — disagreement between an executor's prediction and management's critic-prediction is signal, not error. Escalation triggers: low Jev confidence ∪ verdict-conflict ∪ ambiguity ∪ materiality ∪ random audit — versioned policy, never acceptance-gating (Jev confidence ≈ max-prob; weak alone). One adjudication per root score — no recursive management reviews.

5 · Obligation lifecycle

Scoring state ≠ outcome state — a down scorer never turns confirmed into unresolved. Cell's model loop never drives retries. Exactly-once persistence; disclosed at-most-once-paid billing (crash-after-response may repeat a paid call).
