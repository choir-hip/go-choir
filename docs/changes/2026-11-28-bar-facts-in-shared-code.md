What: in two commits. First, dead Swift deleted: `LaunchTimings` (the `AUTORADIO launch ...` line; nothing reads it) with its marks, `PreparedAudio`, `synthesizeForCache`, `RadioBridge.airAddress()` and the `airAddress` field of the `radioSet` push. Second (S4), the bar's title, playing or paused and progress are made in shared code, on both phones. New `runOf` in `AutoradioLab/radio-follow.ts` (pure; every value read is an argument) makes `barRun` from the sheet's on-air entry, the deck's state (`ds`) and the on-air entry (`rair`, now declared in spine.contract beside `brun`): the deck speaks for the entry on air only when its item is that entry's id, else nothing plays; playing, paused (listener or system) and held for the audio session are the deck's own `why`; progress is the place's share of the text handed to the deck (`nowOf`) for spoken entries and seconds over length for audio, and 0 when the place is another entry's or other text. `bar.ts` reads `ready`, `playing`, `paused`, `waiting`, `title`, `progress` from that and no longer a run overlay. Native `barFacts` is the voice's facts only: `at`, `phase`, `transcript`, `message`, `handsFree`, `holding`, `revisedAt`, `errorAt`, `errorStage`, `errorMessage`. Gone from `interface/bar.json` and `exact.json`: `BarFacts.blockIndex`, `blockCount`, `blockFraction`, `running`, `playing`, `title` and `RadioInfo.title`, `playing` (`RadioInfo` is `level`, `speedValue`, `micLevel`). Deleted in Swift: `BlockClock`, `BarTracker.positionSource`, `blockFraction`, `clockProgress` (and the words-per-second estimate behind them), its wiring in `LabRuntime`, the player and document the radio view was built from, and the 0.4 s timer's touch of `radioView` and `barFacts`. `BarState.swift` 161 to 115 lines; non-voice native lines 2826 to 2777. The bar's play button and the line above it read `bi.playing` and `bi.text`, not `ri.playing` and `ri.title`.

Why: the bar's facts came from Swift, so on Android the bar showed nothing for a story the radio was reading (it answered `barFacts` and `radioView` "not supported" and took the declared default), and what it said on the iPhone came from the programme player's own title and an estimate, not from the entry on air. Native now answers only what the voice or audio engine alone knows.

Proof: the tests were written first and seen red against the old source (`runOf` missing; `bar.test.ts`, `radio-info-fields.test.ts`, `radio-page.test.ts`, `radio-churn.test.ts` and S19-bar failing), then green. `bar-run.test.ts` (G1 to G8), `bar.test.ts` (rewritten to the new facts), `account.test.ts` S19-bar, `radio-churn.test.ts` and `radio-position.test.ts` moved to the new resource arguments; the whole `bun test` (1,926 pass; the one failure was the contract build, fixed by a missing `use` in spine.contract, and `symbol-platform.test.ts` passes after it), the same run as CI does it with no Exact (1,905 pass, 2 skip, 0 fail), the contract build (exit 0), `exact test web` (46 pass; the two `voices.test.contract` failures that exist on main), the Swift suite (867 tests, 0 failures, MailSeal K3 skipped), the Kotlin JVM tests (31, 0 failures; no Kotlin changed), the gates. Not proved: any of it on a device; the iPhone compile of `AutoradioModule+Bar.swift` (Exact compiles it into the app) and `LabRuntime.swift`'s copy in the app module. No Kotlin changed.

Differences a person sees. iPhone: the bar names the entry the sheet has on air and not the programme player's segment; after a skip it is idle for a beat until the deck is on the new entry (it never shows the old title or the old progress); the bar's progress is the share of the entry's text, not the block index, so it moves the same way as the Autoradio page's line; a deck held for the engine (`held_engine`) shows idle. Android: the bar now shows title, playing or paused and progress for a spoken entry and for audio; it shows no voice states, no hands-free state and no errors, because Android has no voice turn.

Remaining native ops of the bar, each with its Android answer (all "NotSupportedError", which Exact turns into the declared default, so nothing throws): `barFacts` (voice facts: default is a phone with no voice turn, correct); `radioView` (yield level, speed, microphone level: default `play`, 1, 0, correct); `radioSpeed` (applies a speed to the audio engine: Android answers not supported, so the speed row of the Autoradio page does nothing on Android); `choirHandsFreeToggle` (opens or closes the microphone: Android has no hearing models ready, so the bar's microphone button opens Voice and models; if they were ready it would do nothing); `choirAsk` and `choirTalkCancel` (start and stop a voice turn: the bar's field accepts a question on Android and nothing answers it). The last three are the decision this change does not make: either Kotlin answers them (the voice turn is the next slice) or the bar and the page do not offer them on Android. Recorded for the owner.

Replaced: the native title, playing flag and block-index progress of the bar; the run-sheet overlay in `bar.ts` for remote audio.

Undo: revert the second commit; the first is independent.

Review round (same change, one more commit). The bar's run is now ONE snapshot: `radioAir` returns the on-air entry's id, address, handed text, title and audio flag from one step, and `runOf(ds, air)` reads nothing else; `barRun` reads no runtime state at all. The deck speaks for the entry only when `ds.item === air.id`; any other pairing (a skip or revision that reached one answer first) gives no title, no progress, nothing playing on the bar's state. Resources against the rule (every value read is an input):
- `ds` = deckState(radioTick): native source, read per radio tick.
- `rpulse` = radioPulse(ds, rnote.tick, radioTick): reads the deck state, the note, the clock tick, the runtime (the pump's own).
- `rair` = radioAir(rpulse.tick, posKey, ds.item, signedOutTick, acs.state): reads the runtime's on-air entry, its title and audio flag, what was handed to the deck; asked again on the pulse, the beat, a change of the deck's item, a sign-out and an account-state change.
- `brun` = barRun(rpulse.tick, posKey, ds, rair, signedOutTick, acs.state): pure in `ds` and `rair`; the other four only ask it again.
- `rpfacts` = radioPageFacts(rpulse.tick, posKey, ds, rair): reads the runtime's sheet and view (the page's own facts, not the bar's), `ds`, `rair`.
- `rfollow` = readerFollowShared(ds, rair, address, ids, texts): pure in its arguments.
- `bi` = barInfo(bf, brun, cvraw, barTick|signedOutTick): pure in its arguments plus the account generation (the held message).
- `rp` = radioPage(rpfacts, bf, cvraw): pure.
- `mutation rpm` now refreshes `rpfacts, rair, brun, rp, rpulse`.
Mid-skip edge: while the deck still sounds the old item and the sheet has the new one on air (until the deck's next state and `rair` agree: one pulse, about 1 s in front, up to 5 s on the slow clock), the bar's state is idle with no title, and the play button says Pause, because a tap sends `radioPlayPause`, whose `toggle` (radio-runtime.ts:131-136) reads the deck's own state and pauses a playing deck. A new fact `sounding` says so. No "between items" state exists to reuse. Tests: G9 and G10 in `bar-run.test.ts`, S19-bar (the next `rair` ask is empty and the contract keys carry the sign-out and account state), churn C1.
