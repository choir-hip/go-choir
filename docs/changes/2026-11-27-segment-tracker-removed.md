title: SegmentTracker removed; the player's tests assert on the player
class: orange
---
What: `mobile/AutoradioKit/Sources/AutoradioKit/SegmentTracker.swift` (168 lines) is deleted, with its nine feed lines in `LiveAudio.swift` (`ProgrammePlayer.playback`, the `paused`, `documentReplaced`, `unitBegan`, `unitEnded` and `cut` calls) and its three test classes (`SegmentTrackerTests`, `RepairTrackerTests`, `Repair2TrackerTests`, 12 tests). Eleven player tests in `SegmentPlayerTests`, `RepairPlayerTests` and `Repair2PlayerTests` that read the player through the tracker now assert on what the player publishes: the blocks that began on air (`programmeAudioStart` in the event log), `onRanDry`, `onPaused`, `onLevel`, `blockIndex`, `userPaused`, `level` and the document. They keep their names. The native-boundary ceiling falls from 34 files, 3375 lines to 33 files, 3206 lines.
Why: the tracker had no production reader (`playback.observe` was called only from tests; `BarTracker` is another type), yet it counted as non-voice native code and kept about 30 tests alive. The move to Exact cannot finish while unread Swift counts against it.
Proof: from `mobile/AutoradioKit`, through the lock, `swift test --filter 'SegmentPlayerTests|RepairPlayerTests|Repair2|SegmentTrackerTests|RepairTrackerTests'`: baseline on origin/main `Executed 32 tests, with 0 failures`; the rewritten player tests on the unchanged sources (tracker still present, `Repair2Stage` excluded) `Executed 29 tests, with 0 failures`; after removal the whole package `swift test`: `Executed 837 tests, with 2 tests skipped and 0 failures` (849 before, less the 12 tracker-only tests). `python3 -I scripts/check-autoradio-native-boundary` reports 33 files, 3206 lines, 17 names; its selftest passes. Not proved on a device.
Replaced: the tracker's events (`segmentBegan`, `blockBegan`, `finished`, `skipped`, `rewound`, `superseded`, `failed`, `paused`, `resumed`, `cut`, `cutEnded`) and its labels as a test seam. The labels `skipped`, `rewound`, `superseded` and `failed` were visible only through the tracker; the player behaviour under them (the skip, the replay, the replaced document, the run that ends dry with no sound) is asserted on the player.
Undo: revert the commit; the tracker, its feed lines, its tests and the old ceiling come back together.
