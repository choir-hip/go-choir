title: sign-out closes the microphone and the turn before the key goes
class: red

What: sign-out now has one epoch and one gate. `ChoirApp.signOut` shuts `TurnGate` as its first statement, synchronously, before any Task: the sign-out epoch moves and the identity of the key that is leaving is remembered. Every turn entry (typed, hands-free commit, button) captures the epoch and asks `admits` again at the last point before anything leaves the phone (after the transcript or `spoken()` wait, in `runTurn`, at the start of the pipeline, on every answer applied), and `ChoirClient.request`, the one place a native request reads the key, refuses while the account signs out. `LabRuntime.closeVoiceForSignOut` then ends the engine's listening and turn within one actor turn before any await (`TurnEngine.endForSignOut`: hands-free off, enrolment tap cleared, turn and pipeline cancelled, voice cut), drops a take in hand, and moves the session off the microphone, bounded to 2 s: a move that fails or does not answer is not waited for and the microphone is closed the direct way (`LabRuntime.stop`) before the wait for shared code. `HandsFreeSwitch.set(true)` refuses unless the account is open, so a take's give-back (which sets it directly) cannot reopen the microphone. The key step is `LabRuntime.endSignOut`: it deletes the key it began with and nothing else (a newer key is kept, and so are its stores), then the sign-out sequence is over. The gate's rule: shut from the first statement of sign-out until its sequence has finished, and afterwards open exactly when a key is stored whose identity differs from the one that left (a key that could not be deleted, or the old key written back late, leaves it shut); it is pure to read. `OnboardingController.init` wires it once at launch (`TurnGate.wire()`, reading the app's key store at each read; unwired, as in the engine alone, tests and the lab, it reads no key and stays open). A typed turn is refused only for sign-out, not during a screen's hold. Not changed: where the key lives, the server client's shape, what the wipe holds, shared code's sign-out.

Why: the first version of this change (b7a48d19f) was sent back by two reviewers: the take's give-back turned hands-free on after sign-out, a turn suspended in transcription or before `runTurn` resumed past the gate and sent with the old key, the gate could stay shut after a sign-in during the wipe, a read of the gate could reopen it, the gate shut only after a Task hop, a sign-in during the wait had its key deleted, and a failing or hung session move left the microphone open. A kill leaves: before the key is deleted, signed in with the microphone off (hands-free is never kept across a launch) and shared code's pending marker finishes the sign-out; after it, signed out, and the marker finishes the wipe. There is no native "sign-in completed" call (a sign-in is shared code writing the Exact secret), so reopening compares who is signed in with who left; the smallest honest event would be a shared-code call into native after the key is written, which is not built.

Proof: `mobile/AutoradioKit/Tests/AutoradioKitTests/SignOutOrderTests.swift`, 19 tests (7 from the first round and 12 new), order observed through one sequence shared by the key store, the session owner, the desk and the voice. The 12 new ones failed on b7a48d19f in the ways named in the failure list at the head of the new section (take give-back reopened the switch, a suspended commit and a suspended typed turn sent to the desk, the new key was deleted, the old key written back reopened the gate, a failing session move left the microphone unreleased and a hung one hung sign-out, a typed turn was refused during a hold) except two that already held (the key that cannot be deleted, a turn right after `signOut()` is called). Mutation check, tests that fail with one part removed: the switch's refusal (take give-back); the typed turn's post-wait check (typed-suspended); the key-read check in `ChoirClient` (the client test); the identity rule, the keyReplaced rule and the end of the sequence (three tests each); the bounded session move and its direct close (two tests); the turn cancel (four tests); the cancel plus the answer checks together (the answer test). Removing the runTurn guard alone, the commit guard alone, or the pipeline-start guard alone fails nothing, because the others still catch it (defence in depth); removing all four engine guards fails the commit and typed tests. Whole suite through the lock: 847 executed, 2 skipped, 0 failures (baseline 828 plus 19). The native non-voice count stays at its ceiling, 3127 lines. Not proved on a device: on a phone, sign out while it is listening and while it is answering, and while a take is recorded.

Replaced: a sign-out that closed the voice inside the wipe, after the key; the first round's `sealed`/`keyGone` gate whose getter mutated.

Undo: revert this commit and b7a48d19f.
