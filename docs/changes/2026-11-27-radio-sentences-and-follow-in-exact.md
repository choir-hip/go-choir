What: native `radioFacts` now hands over facts only, in one answer taken at one moment and read once a pulse by the radio (no second call): the document being spoken (`document`), a key that changes with the block or its words (`blockKey`), its number and the count (`block`, `blocks`), the audio's place in it (`blockFraction`), and the spoken words only when the key is new to shared code (it sends `have`). The reader's follow is refused unless that document is the entry on air. `readerFollow` is asked again on the pulse, the page shown, the document's state and version, and the account state, and a document action refreshes it. The sentence split, the sentence the place falls in, the programme progress and the block and sentence of the page in front that the radio is saying are made in `mobile/AutoradioLab/radio-follow.ts` (cases in `radio-follow.test.ts`), on the one splitter the reader uses. `readerFollow` is a shared source (`radio-bridge.ts`), asked with the radio's own pulse, and no longer a native op; the hand-over of the page to the voice no longer carries sentences. `RadioInfo` across the boundary shrinks from 24 fields to the 5 shared code reads (title, playing, level, speedValue, micLevel). Swift deleted: `BlockClock` and the play-time estimate (the audio position is always set in production), `BarTracker.sentences/sentenceIndex/speedName/clockProgress`, `ReaderFollow` (a three-line shell stays only because `AccountWipe.swift` still names it), `ReaderFeed`, `HeldPages.normalised/all/body`, `PlaybackSpeed.name`, and the tests of only those.

Why: the phone app is Exact and only voice is native; these were logic (names, sentence splitting, progress arithmetic, matching words to blocks) computed in Swift for shared code to read back. What stays native is plain fact: the microphone level, the speed the player is at, the audio's own position, the document only native holds, the engine's events.

Proof: `bun test` in `mobile/AutoradioLab` (counts in the landing report), `bun exact.mjs contract build app.contract` (warnings only), `bun exact.mjs test web` (only the two kokoro failures), `swift test` of `mobile/AutoradioKit` through the lock, the boundary and entry gates. `radio-follow.test.ts` runs the real path (radio on air, native's answers faked, the reader's held page): lit block and sentence, paused, page not held, wipe and relight, a document replaced mid-play, another entry's words, one native read a pulse. Four of its behaviours were broken on purpose and failed. Not proved: a device build. A device must show the reader lighting the sentence the radio is saying, going dark on pause and on sign-out, and following a reload.

Replaced: the Swift sentence split, sentence index, progress, `ReaderFollow`/`ReaderFeed` and the native `readerFollow` op. The radio's sentences are now cut by the reader's regex splitter, not the platform's, so a split may differ from before at an odd abbreviation.
