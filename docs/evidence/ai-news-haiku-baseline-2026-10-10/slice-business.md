## In-window items (most important first)

### 1. Google signs a 20-year nuclear power purchase agreement with Constellation (about 890 MW new-build, 3.6 GW total)
- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Headline:** Google signs a 20-year PPA with Constellation Energy covering 890 MW from 11 nuclear plants in Illinois, Pennsylvania and New Jersey, within a larger 3.59 GW package.
- **Substance:** Constellation said it will invest more than $4.3 billion to upgrade systems at those 11 reactors. A separate 15-year deal covers 2.7 GW from Constellation's existing PJM fleet. Electricity starts reaching the PJM grid as soon as 2028. Bloomberg had reported the deal at roughly $1 billion ahead of the announcement, but no official contract value was disclosed. The agreement also includes a Google Cloud / Gemini Enterprise tie-in for plant operations.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.energyconnects.com/news/renewables/2026/october/google-constellation-energy-strike-deal-for-nuclear-power/ (opened; confirms the date, 20-year PPA, 890 MW, $4.3B)
  - https://tribune.net.ph/2026/10/06/google-nears-1-b-nuclear-power-deal (pre-announcement, Bloomberg-sourced, ~$1B)
  - https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026100706441003641 (Oct 7 corroboration)
- **Confidence:** high on date and capacity. Medium on whether the 890 MW is new reactor capacity or uprates: sources conflict. Low on the dollar value.

### 2. Marvell raises its long-range AI custom-silicon targets at its Oct 6 Investor Day

### 3. TSMC reports record September revenue and a Q3 beat
- **Date:** 2026-10-08 (monthly revenue release)
- **Headline:** TSMC's September 2026 revenue is NT$511.86B, up 54.6% year on year, and Q3 revenue beats the top of guidance.
- **Substance:** September revenue was US$16.1B, slightly below August's record NT$514.81B (down 0.6% month on month). Q3 revenue reached NT$1.494T, up 17.6% quarter on quarter and 50.9% year on year. It beat the top of the US$44.6B to $45.8B guidance range. Nine-month revenue was NT$3.899T, up 41.1%. Management has said demand for leading-edge and 2nm capacity still exceeds supply.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/news/6454456 (opened; published 2026-10-08 16:53)
  - Background: https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202609100014 (opened; this is the August-revenue report, dated Sep 10, so it is not in the window)
- **Confidence:** high on the monthly and Q3 figures (one primary-style news report, internally consistent with the August data). Medium on the 2nm commentary, which comes from the Q2 call.

### 4. Nvidia sets a fresh record high and approaches a $6T market cap

- **Substance:** On Oct 5 the stock closed up 2.12% after an intraday high of $240.10. On Oct 6 it rose as much as about 2% in the morning before giving up some gains.
