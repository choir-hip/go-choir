## Local Adjudication

### Effects and capsule freezing remain out

Cursor's objection is confirmed. The current freeze path in
`internal/agentcore/tools_capsule.go` requires the operation base to equal the
current canonical head, stages a granted release beneath `updater/incoming`, and
advances the self-development operation from executing to frozen. It is coupled
to the rejected effects path, not a harmless supervision projection exercise.

This mission may model and property-test the future bundle bindings, but its
deployed acceptance uses non-effect CoSuper results only. It must not invoke
capsule freeze, verification, acceptance, materialization, checkpoint, or route
actuators.

### Fan-out converges before promotion

`internal/computerevent/reducer.go` admits only one
`PendingTransitionRef`; a second `effect_accepted` refuses. Current
`selfdev.Operation` and `CapsuleEffectBundle` also bind one operation, one
bundle digest, and one exact base head. There is no accepted multi-bundle merge
authority.

Therefore fan-out workers produce independently grounded results, not
independently promotable desired states. Super reconciles the results. In the
future effects-on successor, a bounded integrator CoSuper constructs one new,
content-addressed composed bundle at the then-current base head; independent
verification covers both its inputs and composed behavior. Exactly that bundle
is proposed and may be owner-accepted. Overlap, composition failure, or a head
advance opens a rebase/recomposition obligation; no bundle is silently merged
or directly landed stale.

### Event schema is an in-bound operational decision

The owner settled the role, authority, one-tape, and effects-OFF boundaries.
Choosing the smallest typed schema that implements them is an operational
architecture refinement, provided it passes the Definition's formal,
caller-cutover, privacy, reconstruction, and independent-review gates. The
mission's first slice freezes that vocabulary before implementation. It does
not need another owner decision unless the candidate changes those settled
boundaries.
