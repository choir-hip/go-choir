Second run of `scripts/autopaper_source_audit.py --since 7d`, diffed against
the first run in `../autopaper-source-audit-2026-11-10/` (`diff.md`).
2,100 stories in the 7-day window; 215 are new since the first run.

What moved, for the stories new since the first run:

- **Sections.** The ten-section list is in use: on 2026-11-10 stories carry
  `us`, `politics`, `culture`, `sports`, `science`, `health` and `climate`
  beside `world`, `business` and `tech_ai`, and none is unsectioned that day.
  `us_politics` is gone. `world` still holds 59% of the 24-hour window.
- **Length.** Share under 300 words fell from 91% to 81%, and under 150 words
  from 36% to 29%. Most stories are still short.
- **Adjacent duplicate cite markers.** Share fell from 13.6% to 9.8%.
- **Unsectioned share** of the new stories reads 6.5%, against 0.6% before;
  the 46 unsectioned stories in the 24-hour window are all dated 2026-11-09,
  so this is the window moving, not new stories losing sections.

What this run does not show: whether the changes are caused by lanes B and Q
(landed 2026-11-10 about 04:00Z) or by the day's news mix. A week of data
under the new rules is the test (handoff section 6, item 9).
