**Verdict: SEND_BACK**

The manifest, package export, and generator wiring bind `@choir/autoradio/android` cleanly without altering Apple or web behavior, and `later()` replies once on every path. However, there are five blocking defects in [AutoradioModule.kt](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/exact-package/android/AutoradioModule.kt) around event binding order, WebView error/renderer handling, view destruction while attached, and URL validation/routing strictness.

### Blocking Findings

1. **[AutoradioModule.kt:44-47](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/exact-package/android/AutoradioModule.kt#L44-L47), [116-129](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/exact-package/android/AutoradioModule.kt#L116-L129) — `view()` calls `show()` before `PackageView.events()` is bound, dropping initial `"failed"` events**
   - **Concrete failure case:** In Exact's [Natives.kt:620-652](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/qa-mac/mobile/.exact-src/host/android/canvas/src/main/java/dev/exact/canvas/Natives.kt#L620-L652), `module.create(...)` (`AutoradioModule.view`) runs *before* `bindEvents(e)` (`PackageView.events` in [Extensions.kt:736-743](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/qa-mac/mobile/.exact-src/host/android/canvas/src/main/java/dev/exact/canvas/Extensions.kt#L736-L743)), and `apply(e, props)` (`AutoradioModule.update`) runs immediately after `bindEvents(e)`. When `web-page` is created with an invalid or non-`http(s)` `page` prop (or if `loadUrl` synchronously triggers `onPageStarted`), `view()` calls `show(page)`, sets `loaded = next`, and calls `emit("message", "failed")` while `emits[made]` is still `null`, silently dropping the event. The subsequent `update()` call hits `if (next == loaded) return` at line 118, so `"failed"` is never delivered and `webBusy` ([members-reader.contract:82,89-90](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/AutoradioLab/members-reader.contract#L82-L90)) remains stuck `true`.
   - **Smallest repair:** Remove `.also { it.show(props.optString("page")) }` from `AutoradioModule.view` at line 46 so initial loading runs in `update()` after `PackageView.events()` is bound (matching `Natives.kt`'s `apply` immediately after `bindEvents`).

2. **[AutoradioModule.kt:105-112](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/exact-package/android/AutoradioModule.kt#L105-L112) — `onPageFinished` fires after `onReceivedError`, emitting `"done"` and `"load"` on failed loads**
   - **Concrete failure case:** On Android `WebView`, when a main-frame load fails (offline, DNS failure, or cleartext `http://` blocked at `targetSdk` 36 per [AndroidManifest.xml:16-18](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/qa-mac/mobile/.exact-src/host/android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml#L16-L18)), Chromium calls `onReceivedError` (emitting `"failed"`) and then commits its error page and calls `onPageFinished`, which unconditionally emits `emit("message", "done")` and `emit("load", "")`. On Apple ([AutoradioModule+Reader.swift:52-54](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/exact-package/apple/AutoradioModule+Reader.swift#L52-L54)), `didFinish` does not run after `didFail`/`didFailProvisionalNavigation`.
   - **Smallest repair:** Track `private var mainFrameFailed = false` on `PageWebView` (reset to `false` in `onPageStarted` and `show()`, set to `true` in `onReceivedError` when `request.isForMainFrame`), and only emit `"done"` and `"load"` in `onPageFinished` when `!mainFrameFailed`.

3. **[AutoradioModule.kt:88-113](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/exact-package/android/AutoradioModule.kt#L88-L113) — Missing `onRenderProcessGone` kills the entire app process if the WebView renderer terminates**
   - **Concrete failure case:** `WebViewClient.onRenderProcessGone` defaults to returning `false`. If Android kills or crashes the WebView renderer (e.g., low memory on the device while audio is playing or a heavy page is open), the OS terminates the entire `Autoradio` process instead of letting the app recover (see [Natives.kt:1085-1089](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/qa-mac/mobile/.exact-src/host/android/canvas/src/main/java/dev/exact/canvas/Natives.kt#L1085-L1089)).
   - **Smallest repair:** Override `onRenderProcessGone(view: WebView, detail: RenderProcessGoneDetail): Boolean` in `PageWebView`'s `WebViewClient` to emit `emit("message", "failed")`, tear down the dead `WebView`, and return `true`.

4. **[AutoradioModule.kt:19-29](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/exact-package/android/AutoradioModule.kt#L19-L29), [120-128](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/exact-package/android/AutoradioModule.kt#L120-L128) — URL validation and `page`-prop routing are weaker than `SourceLink.swift` and `WebPageView.setProps`**
   - **Concrete failure cases:**
     - `Uri.parse(text)` at line 22 accepts malformed URLs that Swift's `URL(string: trimmed)` ([SourceLink.swift:15](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/AutoradioKit/Sources/AutoradioKit/SourceLink.swift#L15)) rejects as `.refused`—for example `"https://ex ample.org/x"`, `"https://example.org/a b"`, `"https://example.org\\@evil.com"`, `"http://[bad"`, and `"mailto:a@b\nc"`. All of these pass `route()` as `inApp` or `external` on Android.
     - In `PageWebView.show` (lines 123-126), `LinkRoute.external` calls `openExternal(uri)` before emitting `"failed"`, whereas Apple's `WebPageView.setProps` ([AutoradioModule+Reader.swift:41](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/exact-package/apple/AutoradioModule+Reader.swift#L41)) refuses any non-`inApp` `page` prop with `"failed"` and never launches an external app from `page`.
   - **Smallest repair:** In `route()`, reject strings containing whitespace or ISO control characters (or backslashes/unparsed authority) and validate syntax with `java.net.URI(text)` (or strict host/authority checks matching RFC 3986) before checking `scheme`/`host`. In `PageWebView.show()`, treat `LinkRoute.external` the same as `LinkRoute.refused` (`emit("message", "failed")` without calling `openExternal`).

5. **[AutoradioModule.kt:131-134](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/exact-package/android/AutoradioModule.kt#L131-L134) — `destroyPage()` calls `WebView.destroy()` while still attached to `Natives`**
   - **Concrete failure case:** In [Natives.kt:1449-1452](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/qa-mac/mobile/.exact-src/host/android/canvas/src/main/java/dev/exact/canvas/Natives.kt#L1449-L1452), `module?.destroy(e.view)` (`destroyView` -> `destroyPage()`) is invoked *before* `removeView(e.view)`. Calling `WebView.destroy()` while the `WebView` is still attached to `Natives` violates Android's `WebView.destroy()` contract (`WebView.destroy() called while still attached!`) and runs detach callbacks after destruction.
   - **Smallest repair:** In `destroyPage()`, detach from parent first (`(parent as? android.view.ViewGroup)?.removeView(this)`), replace `webViewClient = WebViewClient()`, then call `stopLoading()` and `destroy()`.

### Checklist Review Summary

1. **`ExactExtension` contract & single reply:** `AutoradioModule(context: ExtensionContext) : ExactExtension(context)` matches `ExactExtensions` (`ExactExtension` constructor and view dispatch). `later()` replies synchronously once on `"readerOpenExternal"` and `"NotSupportedError"` otherwise. View lifecycle (`view()`/`update()`/`destroyView()`) fails on `view()` emitting before `PackageView.events()` binds (Blocking Finding 1) and `destroy()` while attached (Blocking Finding 5).
2. **URL routing vs `SourceLink.swift`:** Schemes (`http`/`https` with non-empty host -> `inApp`; `mailto`/`tel`/`sms`/`facetime` -> `external`; else `refused`) and subframe handling (`!request.isForMainFrame` -> allow `refused` in subframes, hand off `external`, keep `inApp`) match, except `Uri.parse` admits malformed URLs and `PageWebView.show` launches external intents on `page` prop (Blocking Finding 4).
3. **WebView settings, navigation, errors, reuse, destruction:** `javaScriptEnabled = true`, `allowFileAccess = false`, `allowContentAccess = false`, `mixedContentMode = MIXED_CONTENT_NEVER_ALLOW`, `setSupportMultipleWindows(false)` (which routes `target="_blank"`/`window.open` into the same `WebView` through `shouldOverrideUrlLoading`, matching Apple's `createWebViewWith`) are safe; `PackageView` does not expose `reset()`, so `Natives` destroys rather than pools `PageWebView`. Missing error/finished guarding (Blocking Finding 2), missing `onRenderProcessGone` (Blocking Finding 3), and attached `destroy()` (Blocking Finding 5) block.
4. **Generator, package export, manifest:** [gen-exact-interface.py:61-65](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/scripts/gen-exact-interface.py#L61-L65), [package.json:6-7](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/exact-package/package.json#L6-L7), and [android/exact.json:1-22](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/exact-package/android/exact.json#L1-L22) match Exact's `androidImplementations` ([typescript.mjs:1278-1284](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/qa-mac/mobile/.exact-src/js/bake/src/typescript.mjs#L1278-L1284)) and `package_views.rs` contracts. `appleImplementations` and `webImplementations` are unaffected.
5. **Tests & missing device proof:** Running `bun test` on [android-package.test.ts](file:///Users/wiz/Dev/go-choir/.lane/trees/android-package/mobile/AutoradioLab/android-package.test.ts) passed (3/3 tests, 19 assertions), but `P3` only greps `AutoradioModule.kt` as text and does not compile or unit-test `AutoradioModule` / `PageWebView` behavior. Blocking device proof still missing on the physical Motorola:
   - Opening a source page (`WebLayer`) and bar web link (`BarWebLayer`): `"loading"` -> spinner on, then `"done"` -> spinner off, page renders and scrolls, and closing returns to the story.
   - In-page link routing (`https://`, `target="_blank"`, `mailto:`/`tel:`/`sms:`, blocked `javascript:`/`file:`/`intent:`) and failed main-frame load (`onReceivedError` / offline / `http://`) clearing `webBusy` without firing `"done"`.
   - `readerOpenExternal` launching the external browser/mail/dialer app and returning `ok: true`, and refusing invalid URLs with `ok: false`.
   - View teardown on close, activity stop/start recreation (`ExactActivity.onStop`/`onStart`), and renderer termination recovery.
