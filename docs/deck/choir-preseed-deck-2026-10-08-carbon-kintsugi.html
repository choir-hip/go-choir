Choir — the automatic computerThe invention / PSA
The invention — prospective self-alignment

Trust is a prediction you can check.
Every consequential job can teach the next.

01 / Before
Commit
Intended action, meaningful non-actions, and predicted consequences.
02 / During
Observe
Authorized behavior, evidence, and eventual results.
03 / After
Compare
Fidelity and calibration where outcomes resolve.
04 / Improve
Teach the next job
Prompts, tools, routing — eventually weights. Held-out test on fresh work.
commit → observe → compare → improve → new task, held-out
Rules still decide what is allowed; PSA measures commitment against reality. Training and reputation need independently resolvable outcomes — the proof must resist easy-question selection, outcome leakage, and self-evaluation gaming.
