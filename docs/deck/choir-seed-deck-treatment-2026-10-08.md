## The investment case in one paragraph

Choir is building the **Automatic Computer**: a persistent, multi-agent environment in which people and organizations maintain their own knowledge, improve agents from experience, and eventually commercialize specialized intelligence through governed agent APIs. Its distinguishing research mechanism, **Prospective Self-Alignment (PSA)**, compares an agent's prospectively recorded intentions and predictions with its subsequent behavior and observed outcomes. Where outcomes can be resolved, the resulting records become evaluation data for improving prompts, tools, routing, and, through customers' preferred training partners, model weights. They can also establish task-specific reputation. Choir enters the market through **premium, high-depth research and media tools for prosumers**, not free horizontal chat. AutoPaper is built as a live public newspaper that anybody can read **from stored, shared artifacts**; original personalized research and generation require paid inference or explicitly capped invitation/beta credits. AutoRadio extends the same retained intelligence to personalized mobile listening and authored audio. Invite under-distributed independent writers with demonstrable work and help them build publishing reach and earned reputation. In parallel, develop enterprise-grade Automatic Computers; later connect prosumer and institutional agents through a market for authorized, paid intelligence. **Each query reveals demand; resolved outcomes enable improvement; reputation improves matching; better matching increases incentives to build better agents.**

## The focused GTM / broader platform tension

The seed-stage entry is **one user type and one expensive job to be done**: the serious independent researcher, analyst, blogger, or writer producing unusually deep work, often without a successful existing subscription business. They buy a research-and-publishing environment and can publish reusable artifacts for others to read. The public audience is **distribution**, not an assumption that Choir will supply millions of free individual AI sessions. Larger teams and enterprises are the natural expansion of the same behavior, with privacy, governance, training data and commercial APIs added as they mature. The deck must not pretend all buyer categories are day-one targets.

**Access and monetization rule:** *Reading a shared result can be free. Asking Choir to produce a new, personalized result is paid.* This applies to custom AutoPaper articles and interactive/custom AutoRadio. The free visitor accesses already-published articles, recorded programs and other shared artifacts; reading/listening incurs ordinary serving costs, **not new model inference**. Beta credits and sponsored invitations are bounded and temporary. No advertising-dependent plan and no paid placement in reputation rankings.

**Business architecture:** three metered prosumer subscription tiers ($35 / $100 / $350 per month), then individually priced enterprise plans; organizational OS subscriptions, first-party intelligence/data licensing, and **net** commissions on third-party agent transactions. Consumers and organizations are not obliged to buy from each other: prosumers generate attention, expertise and demand; organizations contribute licensed specialist agents and services; both enlarge the pool of experience underlying prospective reputation.

**Not yet claimed:** reliable unattended multi-agent autonomy, a shipped participatory AutoRadio product, enterprise production readiness, an independently proven PSA-performance moat, secure commercial agent-query marketplace, strategic data customers, or current consumer scale. AutoRadio's **1–2 month** estimate is a development target, not a customer promise. Enterprise OS availability requires nonfunctional engineering and an independent security audit.

## 04 / 14 — PROSPECTIVE SELF-ALIGNMENT

**Headline:** **An agent should do what it says it will.**

**PSA means Prospective Self-Alignment.** Before an authorized action, a system records intended behavior, meaningful non-actions, and anticipated consequences. Later, the system compares what it said with what it did and what actually happened. It is *self*-alignment because the agent's own **prior commitments** become part of the empirical standard; *prospective* because they were recorded before the outcome was known.

Three columns: **COMMIT** / **OBSERVE** / **COMPARE**.

**Footer:** *External rules still decide what's allowed. PSA adds measurable consistency between commitment, action and consequence.*

**Important precision:** Completing an authorized action and forecasting its consequences are distinct evaluation dimensions. A prohibited action never becomes permissible simply because an agent promised to take it.

# APPENDIX & DILIGENCE NOTES

## A. The two mutually reinforcing adoption loops

**Prosumer / public loop:** premium deep research and personalized audio → reusable published articles, clips and broadcasts → free stored-content consumption and distribution → discovery of independent experts → paid research producers and opted-in prospective records → better reputation and relevant coverage. Serving shared content costs bandwidth/storage, not a fresh inference run per recipient.

**Organizational / commercial loop:** private knowledge → owned Automatic Computer → internal PSA records → better tools/agents/model choices → authorized sellable agent API → paid questions reveal demand → outcomes (when resolvable) improve capability and reputation → investment in data and training. Enterprises add specialist supply to the network; prosumers provide external discovery, expertise and user demand. There is no need for mandatory cross-side transactions.

## B. Paid inference unit economics

Charge for generating personalized intelligence, not reading an existing artifact. **Working tiers:** $35/month Research, $100/month Studio, and $350/month Professional, followed by custom-priced enterprise contracts. The $350 tier serves individual power researchers and producers with materially larger inference budgets and sophisticated workflows; enterprise adds organizational governance, reliability and deployment requirements. All three individual tiers have defined allowances and capabilities, *not* unlimited deep research. Heavy inference is metered via credits/top-ups or plans with stricter quotas. Track inference cost per paid user, source retrieval and verification costs, payment fees, hosted compute, audio/TTS, storage/CDN and chargebacks. A trial invitation can have a hard compute budget. The companion model contains adjustable per-tier monthly cost assumptions (including an illustrative $140/month cost for the $350 plan); actual unit economics and paid-tier distribution remain unvalidated.

## C. PSA as both self-alignment and self-supervision

Normative instruction-following, policies and human governance stay external. Self-alignment compares actions and consequences with the agent's own *pre-outcome* representations, generating an auditable mismatch. Where outcomes can be independently resolved, records can train evaluators, prompt search, context / routing optimization and eventually model weights. Reputation uses longitudinal, task-specific, difficulty-aware evidence rather than paid priority or a single scoreboard.

## D. Agent APIs and private data

An agent API can sell **authorized outputs** rather than a database dump, but outputs may leak private data or permit inference attacks across repeated calls. Sellers must control scope, use, retention, training/redistribution rights, pricing, query budgets, logging and audit. Third-party buyers' queries can themselves be sensitive commercial data; they are not silently added to global training sets. Secure marketplaces and enterprise isolation remain future build requirements.

## E. Market sizing and financing

Present the market via mechanics, **not** additive whole-industry TAM: paid prosumers × ARPU; enterprises × ACV; separately licensed data/training contracts; external marketplace paid calls × transaction value × net take. Retention, spend, heavy-tail usage and distribution are key uncertainties.

The attached **one-sheet financial model** holds an explicitly ambitious founder-scale case. It should not be presented as a validated growth projection or as proof that $1 million alone finances those targets. It models Q4 2026 seed financing and illustrative later raises in 2027–2029. Historical 2026 results, current traction and committed capital must be filled in from actual records before any factual declaration to investors or QDB.

## F. Demonstration roadmap

1. AutoPaper publishes valuable maintained coverage as a true public demo; visitors can read existing artifacts freely.
2. Restricted paid/beta custom research with inference limits and a measured cost per useful output.
3. Invitation-only under-distributed writer cohort; historical evidence evaluation with appropriate methodological caution.
4. AutoRadio mobile MVP; personalized audio behind paid limits, freely shared recorded programs.
5. Held-out PSA evaluation showing useful prospective data improves performance or agent matching.
6. Enterprise reliability/security gates and audited, bounded organizational pilots.
7. Paid specialist-agent API with explicit disclosure limits and controlled settlement.

**Final takeaway:** *Choir helps people and organizations own intelligence, improve it from experience, and sell authorized access to what it knows. PSA supplies the training signal and reputation record that can make a marketplace for intelligence trustworthy.*
