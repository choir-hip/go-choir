Phase 1 · the technology

The automatic computer.

A persistent computer that works continuously and develops itself. An RLM (recursive language model, cf. Zhang et al., MIT) coordinates many communicating agents — persistent and ephemeral — over long horizons. Every change a typed event; every surface a deterministic projection.
RLM provenance continuous unownable by design

The supervision problem

Chat breaks at this scale.

Long-horizon, multi-agent work creates a complexity that breaks the affordances of chat as UX — you can't supervise a continuously running system through a scrollback.

The answer: agents supervise agents, and documents show the current work state. The document is the control surface — and the same surface the product is made of.
choir — multiplayer ai05
Phase 1 · the learning loop

Own your own learning loop.

PICL — predictive in-context learning. Before acting, an agent commits a prediction; after acting, the outcome resolves it. Every committed prediction becomes a provenance-linked record — improving the quality of outcomes through learning, and safety through logging and monitoring.
Security
Encrypted storage, key escrow, owner-scoped.
Auditability
Every change a typed event; full provenance.
Customizability
Vibecode your own apps — multiagent workflows and APIs inside the autoputer.
Recovery
If anything goes wrong you can always recover — your data is safe.

The deeper point
The opportunity after autonomy is coordination.
Most AI systems reduce the human intervention required. Choir increases the human input that can be productively absorbed — a meeting becomes an input to a persistent multi-agent system already doing the work. AI not as a replacement for humans, but as leverage: an organization with higher coordination bandwidth, not higher overhead.

The box score

Of course you keep score.

A provenance-linked object graph already knows who said what — the obvious next layer tracks whether they were right.
Public
The public system tracks the discourse — every statement a forecast with a resolution contract; figures accumulate track records.
Private
The private system tracks internal decision contributions — who moved the work, and whether it held.
Matures into the station's editorial judgment — and its own data product and API.
